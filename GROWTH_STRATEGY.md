# 📈 Retail Growth Strategy Report
**Project:** Retail Marketing Analytics — Customer Segmentation & Growth Strategy  
**Date:** 2024  

---

## Executive Summary

This report translates data-driven customer segmentation and marketing channel analysis into concrete growth recommendations. Using RFM scoring and K-Means clustering on 5,000 retail transactions, we identified five behavioral segments and evaluated six marketing channels across a four-year period.

---

## 1. Customer Segment Strategies

### 🏆 Champions (~10% of customers, highest LTV)
**Who:** Bought recently, buy often, spend the most.  
**Strategy:**
- Launch an invite-only **VIP loyalty tier** (free shipping, priority support, early access)
- Offer **bundle upsells** at checkout tailored to past categories
- Use as brand advocates — referral bonus programme

### 💚 Loyal Customers (~20%)
**Who:** Buy regularly, moderate spend.  
**Strategy:**
- **Cross-category promotions** (e.g., Electronics buyer → Accessories)
- Personalised email digest with "You might also like…" recommendations
- Reward streaks: bonus points for consecutive monthly purchases

### 🌱 Potential Loyalists (~30%)
**Who:** Recent buyers, not yet habitual.  
**Strategy:**
- **Onboarding nurture sequence** — 3-email drip over 30 days post-purchase
- Second-purchase incentive: 10% off next order within 45 days
- App download push with exclusive in-app pricing

### ⚠️ At-Risk Customers (~25%)
**Who:** Used to buy, now drifting away.  
**Strategy:**
- **Win-back campaign**: "We miss you — here's 15% off" with 14-day urgency
- Survey to capture churn reason (price, selection, experience)
- Re-engage through retargeting on social platforms

### 💔 Lost Customers (~15%)
**Who:** Long dormant, minimal probability of return.  
**Strategy:**
- Final automated win-back email (deep discount, last attempt)
- Suppress from expensive paid channels — preserve ad budget
- Analyse exit patterns to prevent future churn

## 2. Marketing Channel Allocation

| Channel | Avg ROI | Avg CAC | Recommendation |
|---------|---------|---------|----------------|
| Email | High | Lowest | **↑ Increase budget** |
| Search Ads | High | Low | **↑ Scale keywords** |
| Social Media | Medium | Medium | Maintain + optimise creative |
| Influencer | Medium | High | Focus on micro-influencers |
| Affiliate | Low-Med | Medium | Renegotiate commission terms |
| TV/Radio | Low | Highest | **↓ Reduce or pilot-test** |

### Budget Reallocation (Suggested)
- Reallocate **15–20% of TV/Radio spend** → Email + Search Ads
- Earmark **5%** for Mobile App channel development (fastest growth vector)

---

## 3. Product & Category Opportunities

- **Electronics** leads revenue — protect with extended warranty upsells
- **Beauty & Groceries** have strong repeat-purchase potential — build subscription bundles
- **Books & Toys** are underperforming relative to margin — consider catalogue pruning or targeted promotions

---

## 4. Seasonal & Regional Tactics

- **East region** drives the highest sales volume — prioritise for new product launches
- Revenue peaks Q4 — begin promotional planning in Q3, ramp ad spend in October
- **Online channel** is growing fastest — invest in UX, personalisation engine, and faster delivery SLAs
